## Conversion is lossy, and can be wrong

Converting a PDF to DOCX is a reconstruction, not a copy. The source format does not
record paragraphs, headings or list nesting, so the converter infers them. Inference
can be wrong even when the output looks right — a paragraph can be mis-levelled, a
table can be reconstructed with the wrong cell boundaries — and the appearance of the
result is not evidence that its structure is correct.

**Do not rely on a converted document as a record of an original.** Keep the source PDF,
and verify any structural claim you depend on.
